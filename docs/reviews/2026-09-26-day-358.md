## How this was done

production runs) with **a year of demo data** loaded by `scripts/seed-year.mjs --before` (the same

declined, 6 lost, 8 done), 12 research briefs, 26 media kit versions, 400 kit views, 51 weeks of
stats, 135 emails, 705 events, and files adding up to **9.05 GB of the 10 GB free storage**.

Every screen walked as Sheila on an iPhone 13 (390 × 664 visible) and on a 1280 desktop by
`scripts/day358-shots.mjs`, which also measured each page's height and rows
(`docs/design/day-358/before/*.jpg`, `measure.json`). The stance: she is not technical, she opens
this on her phone between hosting jobs, and it is day 358. Everything below either **stacks up**
(grows with time and never goes away), **slows down**, **lies** (a count or a light that is
wrong or silently cut off), **buries the next action**, or **can't be cleared**.

Rating: **blocker** (loses her work, fills the storage, or tells her something untrue),
**weak** (works but wears her down), **polish**.

## What "fixed" means (the owner's rules)

- **Archive is not delete.** One tap archives (Home cards: dismiss) with **Undo** in the toast;
  "Show archived" on every list that can archive; Restore puts it back.
- **Automatic tidy rules**, plain ages, on by default (Settings → Tidy up, a switch she can turn
  off): finished dumps archive after 30 days, finished or dead deals after 60 days, deals that
  went quiet (no activity) after 90 days, old research briefs after 90 days, failed voice overs
  after 14 days and unused ones after 60 days. Tidy never deletes a file.
- **Storage rules** (always on, same as the raw and rejected rules): raw originals 7 days after
  cutting and rejected clips 7 days after rejecting (unchanged); **posted clip files 30 days after
  posting unless in the media kit** (the cover, stats and post link stay); **unapproved drafts
  60 days after they were made, with a Home warning 7 days before and one tap Keep**; full videos
  unchanged (7 days after posting, 14 unapproved). A draft is never deleted unless the warning was
  on Home for at least 7 days.
- **A hard budget** (9 GB, 90% of the free 10 GB): the daily lane applies the same rules earlier,
  least harm first, and never touches an approved clip waiting to post, a kit clip, or an unwarned
  draft. Still over: red light and the storage fix guide.
- **Home is one phone screen however much piles up**: every section capped with "See all (N)";
  a validator fails if any Home list can exceed its cap.
- **Long lists page, search and filter; counts stay true** ("12 of 329").
