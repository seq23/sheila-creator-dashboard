## Step 1

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/clips-look-wrong-1.png)

Open **Dump** and look at your latest dump. If it says **Something went wrong**, read the sentence under it: it says what to do.

## Step 2

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/clips-look-wrong-2.png)

If it says the videos couldn't be opened, start a new dump and add the same videos again.

## Step 3

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/clips-look-wrong-3.png)

If it says no usable moments were found, dump longer takes with talking or action. Clips need at least a few seconds of something happening.

## Step 4

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/clips-look-wrong-4.png)

If clips are cut in odd places or framed badly, reject them in **Review** with the reason **Bad cut**. That feeds the next cut.

## Step 5

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/clips-look-wrong-5.png)

If captions or hooks sound off, reject with **Not on brand** or edit them. Then check your Brand Profile in **Client Brain** is up to date and locked.

## Step 6

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/clips-look-wrong-6.png)

Still wrong after two dumps? Tap **No** below and we'll email your helper with the details.
