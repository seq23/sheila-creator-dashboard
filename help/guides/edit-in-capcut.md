The dashboard's own editor makes every clip for free. When you'd rather finish a clip yourself in
CapCut, InShot or any editing app, send it there and upload your edit back: it replaces the clip,
and nothing else changes. CapCut has no way for other apps to connect to it, so there is no key to
paste: this is how you use CapCut instead of the built-in editor.

## Tap Edit in CapCut

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/edit-in-capcut-1.png)
<!-- target: .clip-card >> text=Edit in CapCut -->

Open **Review**. Under the clip, tap **Edit in CapCut**. Pick **CapCut**, **InShot** or **Another
app** at the top.

## Send the clip to CapCut

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/edit-in-capcut-2.png)
<!-- click: .clip-card >> text=Edit in CapCut -->
<!-- target: .handoff-steps -->

On your phone, tap **Share to CapCut** and pick **CapCut** in the share sheet. On a computer, tap
**Save the clip** and open it in CapCut on the web.

## Edit it in CapCut

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/edit-in-capcut-3.png)
<!-- route: external -->

Make your changes. Keep the ratio **9:16** (tall), then tap **Export** and save the video.

## Replace with my edit

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/edit-in-capcut-4.png)
<!-- click: .clip-card >> text=Edit in CapCut -->
<!-- target: label[for="edit-file"] -->

Back in the dashboard, tap **Replace with my edit** and pick the video you exported. We check it
is tall and not longer than the platforms this clip goes to allow (Instagram 90 seconds, YouTube
Shorts 3 minutes, TikTok 10 minutes), then level the sound and make a new cover.

## Wait about a minute

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/edit-in-capcut-5.png)
<!-- target: .clip-card -->

The clip says **Finishing your edit from CapCut, about a minute**, then **Edited in CapCut**.
The old version plays until yours is ready.

## Did this work?

If it says your edit is not a tall 9:16 video, set the ratio to 9:16 in CapCut and export again.
If it is too long for a platform, trim it, or untick that platform on the clip first. The clip is
never changed until your edit passes these checks.
