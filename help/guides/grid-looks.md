Grid looks put several moments in one vertical video. One cell plays its sound and its words
become the captions; the others play muted. Every cell follows the face in its own moment, and
small or low-quality videos are sharpened and never stretched.

## Pick what each cell shows

![Step 7](/help/screenshots/grid-looks-7.png)
<!-- click: .clip-card >> text=Change look -->
<!-- target: [data-look-option="grid_four"] -->

In **Review**, tap **Change look** under a clip and pick a grid. Tap a cell in the preview, then
choose what it shows: **This clip**, this clip **Closer**, another clip **From this dump**, or
one of **Your approved clips**. Tap **Play sound from this cell** on the cell whose voice you
want. Then tap **Make it in this look**.

## Did this work?

The preview changes as you pick. If **Make it in this look** stays grey, keep the clip itself in
at least one cell.
