Once you agree a deal, the dashboard runs the rest like a manager would: confirm it in writing,
send the draft for approval, post with #ad, send their results on day 7, invoice, remind them
if payment is late, and say thank you with a next idea 30 days later.

## Confirm the deal in writing

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/invoice-a-brand-1.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .deal-card >> nth=0 -->
<!-- target: .memo-card -->

Tap **We agreed a deal**, fill in the fee, dates and usage on the **Deal memo**, then send the
**Confirm what's agreed** email. Copy the contract checklist if they send a contract.

## Tick off delivery

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/invoice-a-brand-2.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .deal-card >> nth=0 -->
<!-- target: .delivery-card -->

In **Delivery**, tick each step as it happens: brief, idea agreed, draft sent, approved, posted
with #ad. Paste the link to the post.

## Send their results

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/invoice-a-brand-3.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .deal-card >> nth=0 -->
<!-- target: .email-panel -->

Seven days after posting, **Results report** is written with your real numbers from Stats.
Attach screenshots from the app and send.

## Make the invoice

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/invoice-a-brand-4.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .deal-card >> nth=0 -->
<!-- target: .delivery-card -->

Tap **Make the invoice**, then **Open invoice** and save it as a PDF. Add your payment details
where it says, then send the **Send the invoice** email with the PDF attached.

## Mark it paid

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/invoice-a-brand-5.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .deal-card >> nth=0 -->
<!-- target: .delivery-card -->

If payment is late, the card says so and the reminder is written. When the money lands, tap
**Mark paid**: the brand is added to your media kit's past collaborations.

## Did this work?

If the invoice has no fee, add the agreed fee on the Deal memo first.
