## Step 1

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/log-in-1.png)

Open the dashboard link on your phone or computer.

## Step 2

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/log-in-2.png)

Type your email under **Your email** and tap **Email me a code**.

## Step 3

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/log-in-3.png)

Open the email and find the 6-digit code.

## Step 4

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/log-in-4.png)

Type it under **The code from your email** and tap **Log in**.

## Step 5

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/log-in-5.png)

You stay logged in on that device for 30 days. There is no password to remember.
