Every clip is made in a **look**: its captions, its layout (full frame, a blurred background, a
small inset, or a grid of several moments), a little zoom or a progress bar, and your end card.
Each dump's clips take turns through the looks, so they never all look the same. Nothing costs
anything: the dashboard's own editor makes every look.

## See each clip's look

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-1.png)
<!-- target: .clip-card .look-chip -->

Open **Review**. Under each clip, **Look:** says which look it was made in.

## Tap Change look

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-2.png)
<!-- click: .clip-card >> text=Change look -->
<!-- target: .look-list -->

Under the clip, tap **Change look**. You see every look with a small preview and one line on
what it does.

## Pick a look

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-3.png)
<!-- click: .clip-card >> text=Change look -->
<!-- target: [data-look-option="karaoke"] -->

Tap the one you want. Just this clip is made again in that look, in about a minute. The current
version keeps playing until the new one is ready, then it swaps in by itself. A grid look first
asks what each cell shows; see **The grid looks, picture by picture**.

## Wait about a minute

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-4.png)
<!-- target: .clip-card -->

The clip says **Re-rendering, about a minute**. You can keep reviewing other clips meanwhile.

## Choose which looks are in the mix

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-5.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: section[aria-label="Editing"] -->

Open **Settings**, then **Editing**. Every look is on to start. Untick any you never want and
new dumps skip it. Captions and your end card are on too; switch them off here if you like.

## Add your own music (optional)

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-6.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: label[for="music-file"] -->

Under **My music**, tap **Add a song** and pick an MP3 or M4A you own or have the rights to.
The **Music bed** switches on and new clips play it quietly under your voice. We never add
music you didn't upload: a song you don't hold the rights to can get a post muted or taken
down, so the only music used is yours.

## Did this work?

The new look shows on the clip within a couple of minutes. If it says **The new look didn't
finish**, the clip is unchanged; tap **Change look** again. Clips older than a week can't change
their look because the original video was cleared; dump that video again for new looks.
