Once your voice is saved, type what it should say and attach it to a clip.

## Which voice is in use

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/make-a-voice-over-1.png)
<!-- target: .engine-card -->

**Which voice** says whether the free built-in voice or your ElevenLabs premium voice is used, and why.

## Voice overs on clips

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/make-a-voice-over-2.png)
<!-- target: .clips-switch -->

This switch must be on to add voice overs to clips. Off keeps clips real footage.

## Write the script

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/make-a-voice-over-3.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Draft with AI"] -->

Under **Make a voice over**, type what it should say, or tap **Draft with AI**.

## Tap Generate

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/make-a-voice-over-4.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Generate"] -->

Tap **Generate**. Premium takes seconds; built-in takes a few minutes.

## Premium or built-in

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/make-a-voice-over-5.png)
<!-- target: [data-narration="demo_narr_2"] -->

Each voice over says which voice made it. Tap play to listen.

## Attach it to a clip

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/make-a-voice-over-6.png)
<!-- target: [data-narration="demo_narr_1"] -->

Tap **Attach to a clip** and pick the clip. It plays over that clip when it posts.
