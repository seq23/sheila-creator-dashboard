When a brand answers your pitch, tell the dashboard with one tap. Your follow-up reminders stop
and the deal moves along.

## Find the brand you pitched

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/mark-a-reply-1.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- target: .brand-card >> nth=2 -->

Open **Deals**. Brands you pitched show **Sent** on their card. Follow-ups that are due also
show on **Home**.

## Tap They replied

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/mark-a-reply-2.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .brand-card >> nth=2 -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="They replied"] -->

Tap the brand, then **They replied**. The day-5 and day-12 reminders stop for this brand.

## No reply yet? Send the follow-up

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/mark-a-reply-3.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .brand-card >> nth=2 -->
<!-- target: role=tab[name="Follow-up day 5"] -->

Tap **Follow-up day 5**, then **Open in Gmail** and send it. Come back and tap **I sent the
follow-up**; we'll remind you once more on day 12.

## Move the deal along

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/mark-a-reply-4.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .brand-card >> nth=2 -->
<!-- target: .tracker -->

When you start talking money, tap **We're talking terms**. When you agree, tap **We have a
deal**. Not the right fit? Tap **Pass on it**.

## Add what you owe them

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/mark-a-reply-5.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .brand-card >> nth=2 -->
<!-- target: .tracker -->

On a won deal, add each video you promised with its due date. The clip is marked
**Paid partnership** in Review so the caption gets #ad.

## Did this work?

If the buttons are missing, check the pitch was marked as sent first.
