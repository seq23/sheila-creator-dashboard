Your media kit is the one link in every pitch. Brands see your photo, your numbers (with the
date and where each came from), your best work, the brands you've worked with and your rates.
You edit a draft; nothing is public until you tap **Publish**.

## Open the Media kit tab

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/media-kit-1.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- target: role=tab[name="Media kit"] -->

In **Deals**, tap the **Media kit** tab at the top.

## Fix what the Kit check lists

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/media-kit-2.png)
<!-- route: /deals?tab=kit -->
<!-- target: .kit-check -->

The **Kit check** lists anything missing or out of date: no photo, old numbers, no packages,
too few clips. Tap the button beside each one and it is fixed or you are taken to the spot.

## Fill in your cover

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/media-kit-3.png)
<!-- route: /deals?tab=kit -->
<!-- target: #kit-handles -->

Add a photo, one line under your name, your niche, where you are, and your handles. Everything
saves by itself as you type.

## Check your numbers

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/media-kit-4.png)
<!-- route: /deals?tab=kit -->
<!-- target: .kit-figs -->

Your followers, views and engagement come from your stats with the date and source. A number
the dashboard can't check (like your audience's age) goes in **Add manually**; brands see it
marked "self-reported".

## Set your packages and rates

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/media-kit-5.png)
<!-- route: /deals?tab=kit -->
<!-- target: #kit-packages -->

Each package has a **Starting at** price brands see (or tick **Rates on request**), and a
private target and floor only you see. The suggestion beside each one shows its math and its
source, or says there is no benchmark.

## Preview, then Publish

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/media-kit-6.png)
<!-- route: /deals?tab=kit -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Preview"] -->

Tap **Preview** to see exactly what brands see, then **Publish**. Each publish is a new dated
version; your link always shows the newest one. The bar at the top says how many times your kit
was viewed.

## Did this work?

If your numbers are missing, connect your stats or upload your TikTok export, then come back.
