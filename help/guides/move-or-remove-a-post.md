## Step 1

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/move-or-remove-a-post-1.png)

Tap **Calendar** in the menu.

## Step 2

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/move-or-remove-a-post-2.png)

Find the post you want to change and tap it.

## Step 3

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/move-or-remove-a-post-3.png)

To move it, pick a new day under **Move to…** and tap **Move**. It keeps the same time of day. On a computer you can also drag the post onto another day.

## Step 4

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/move-or-remove-a-post-4.png)

To trade places with another post, pick it under **Swap with…** and tap **Swap**.

## Step 5

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/move-or-remove-a-post-5.png)

To take it off, tap **Take off the calendar**. The clip goes back to **Approved, not scheduled** and stays there until you add it again or tap **Fill the calendar**.

## Step 6

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/move-or-remove-a-post-6.png)

If a day is full you will see a message like "TikTok already has 10 posts that week". Move another post out of that week first, or pick another week.
