Every day the dashboard looks for brands with money for creators like you and ranks them. Each
card says how we know they pay, why now, and who to write to, each with the page it came from.
You send every email yourself from your own Gmail.

## Look at Brands to pitch this week

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-1.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- target: .prospect >> nth=0 -->

Open **Deals**. The top cards pay creators already; tap **How it ranked** to see the math.
Brands with no sign yet that they pay are below the line.

## Tap Pitch

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-2.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- target: .prospect >> nth=0 >> role=button[name="Pitch"] -->

Tap **Pitch**. The deal opens with your first email already written: one idea, your numbers,
three options from your rate card, and your media kit link.

## Read it and make it yours

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-3.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .deal-card >> nth=0 -->
<!-- target: .email-draft -->

Pick a subject line, change any word, and try **Warm**, **Straight** or **Short**. The **Before
you send** list shows anything missing.

## Tap Open in Gmail and send

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-4.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .deal-card >> nth=0 -->
<!-- target: role=link[name="Open in Gmail"] -->

Tap **Open in Gmail**. Gmail opens with the address, subject and email filled in. Press
**Send** in Gmail. If the brand uses a form, the button says **Open their form** and copies
your email to paste.

## Come back and tap Mark sent

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-5.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .deal-card >> nth=0 -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Mark sent"] -->

Tap **Mark sent** and pick the day. Follow-ups come up on day 5, 12 and 19, each written for
you; after the third the card asks you to close it as "no reply".

## Get listed where brands look

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-6.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- target: .listings -->

Under **Get listed here**, open a marketplace to see what it pays and how to join. Tick **I'm
on it** once you have.

## Did this work?

If Gmail did not open, use **Copy** and paste the email into a new message instead.
