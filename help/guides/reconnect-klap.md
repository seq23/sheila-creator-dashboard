Klap's light is red (its key stopped working) or yellow (it is out of credits). Meanwhile the
built-in editor does its job, so nothing waits.

## Check your Klap account

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/reconnect-klap-1.png)
<!-- route: external -->

Log in at [klap.app](https://klap.app). If you are out of credits, top up or wait for your plan to
renew. If the key was deleted, create a new one under **REST API**.

## Paste the new key

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/reconnect-klap-2.png)
<!-- route: /settings/connections -->
<!-- target: role=textbox[name="Editor · Klap key"] -->

Open **Settings**, then **Connections**, **Editing apps**. Tap **Disconnect** on the Klap card,
paste the new key and tap **Check key**.

## Did this work?

The light turns green on **Settings**. Your next dump or clip uses Klap again.
