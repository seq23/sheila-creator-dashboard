## What happened

The AI key stopped working, so new captions and research wait until it is back.

## Step 1

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/reconnect-openrouter-1.png)

Open **OpenRouter** (openrouter.ai) and log in.

## Step 2

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/reconnect-openrouter-2.png)

Tap your picture, then **Keys**.

## Step 3

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/reconnect-openrouter-3.png)

Tap **Create key**, name it Dashboard, and copy it.

## Step 4

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/reconnect-openrouter-4.png)

In the dashboard open **Settings**, then **Connect accounts**.

## Step 5

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/reconnect-openrouter-5.png)

Under **OpenRouter**, paste the key and tap **Check key**.

## Did this work?

If not, tap **No** below and we'll open the matching fix-it guide or email your helper.
