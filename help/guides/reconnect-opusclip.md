Opus Clip's light is red (its key stopped working) or yellow (it is out of credits). Meanwhile the
built-in editor does its job, so nothing waits.

## Check your Opus Clip account

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/reconnect-opusclip-1.png)
<!-- route: external -->

Log in at [opus.pro](https://opus.pro). If you are out of credits, top up or wait for your plan to
renew. If the key was deleted, create a new one under your profile, then **API** (Pro, Max or Business plans).

## Paste the new key

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/reconnect-opusclip-2.png)
<!-- route: /settings/connections -->
<!-- target: role=textbox[name="Editor · Opus Clip key"] -->

Open **Settings**, then **Connections**, **Editing apps**. Tap **Disconnect** on the Opus Clip card,
paste the new key and tap **Check key**.

## Did this work?

The light turns green on **Settings**. Your next dump or clip uses Opus Clip again.
