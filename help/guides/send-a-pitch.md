Every week the dashboard finds brands that fit you and writes a pitch in your words. You read
it, change anything you like, and send it yourself from your own Gmail. The dashboard never
sends anything for you.

## Pick a brand

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/send-a-pitch-1.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- target: .brand-card >> nth=0 -->

Open **Deals** and tap a brand card. The **Fit** number and the line under the name say why it
suits you.

## Check why it fits and who to contact

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/send-a-pitch-2.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .brand-card >> nth=0 -->
<!-- target: .why -->

Read **Why it fits** and **Contact**. Every contact shows the brand's own page it was found on,
so you know it is real.

## Tap Draft a pitch

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/send-a-pitch-3.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .brand-card >> nth=0 -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Draft a pitch"] -->

Tap **Draft a pitch**. In a few seconds you get a subject, an email, a short DM and two
follow-ups.

## Read it and make it yours

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/send-a-pitch-4.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .brand-card >> nth=1 -->
<!-- target: .pitch-text -->

Read the email. Tap into the box to change any word. It saves by itself when you tap outside
the box.

## Tap Open in Gmail

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/send-a-pitch-5.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .brand-card >> nth=1 -->
<!-- target: role=link[name="Open in Gmail"] -->

Tap **Open in Gmail**. Gmail opens with the address, subject and email filled in. Press
**Send** in Gmail. If the brand uses a form, the button says **Open form** and your pitch is
copied so you can paste it.

## Come back and tap Mark as sent

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/send-a-pitch-6.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- click: .brand-card >> nth=1 -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Mark as sent"] -->

Back here, tap **Mark as sent** and pick the day you sent it. We'll remind you on **Home** to
follow up on day 5 and day 12.

## Did this work?

If Gmail did not open, use **Copy** and paste the pitch into a new email instead.
