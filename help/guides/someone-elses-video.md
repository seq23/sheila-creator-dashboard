## Step 1

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/someone-elses-video-1.png)

When we cut your videos we look at a few frames for a TikTok or Instagram watermark. If the name on it is not one of your own accounts in Buffer, we hold that video's clips off your calendar.

## Step 2

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/someone-elses-video-2.png)

Open **Dump** (or the dump's clips in **Review**). The yellow note says which name we saw.

## Step 3

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/someone-elses-video-3.png)

If it really is your own video (an old post of yours, or an account you run), tap **This is my video**. Its clips can go on the calendar straight away.

## Step 4

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/someone-elses-video-4.png)

If it is someone else's video, reject its clips in **Review**. Posting another creator's video can get your account flagged and breaks their copyright.
