Your media kit is the one link that goes in every pitch. Brands see your photo, what you make,
your numbers and your best clips. Your numbers update on their own every week.

## Open the Media kit tab

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/update-your-media-kit-1.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- target: role=tab[name="Media kit"] -->

In **Deals**, tap the **Media kit** tab at the top.

## Add your photo

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/update-your-media-kit-2.png)
<!-- route: /deals?tab=kit -->
<!-- target: .kit-photo-row label.btn -->

Tap **Add a photo** and pick a bright, friendly photo of you from your camera roll.

## Write your one-line bio

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/update-your-media-kit-3.png)
<!-- route: /deals?tab=kit -->
<!-- target: .kit-editor textarea -->

Type one or two lines about who you are and what you make. Keep it short: brands skim.

## Pick your 3 best clips

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/update-your-media-kit-4.png)
<!-- route: /deals?tab=kit -->
<!-- target: .kit-pick -->

Tick up to **3** clips you are proudest of. Only approved clips are listed.

## Save it

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/update-your-media-kit-5.png)
<!-- route: /deals?tab=kit -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Save media kit"] -->

Tap **Save media kit**. Brands see the change straight away.

## See what brands see

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/update-your-media-kit-6.png)
<!-- route: /deals?tab=kit -->
<!-- target: role=link[name="Open my media kit"] -->

Tap **Open my media kit** to check it on your phone. **Download PDF** saves a one-page copy
for brands that ask for a file.

## Did this work?

If your numbers look old, your stats connection may need you. Tap **No** below and we'll open
the fix-it guide.
